# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This code of conduct establishes respectful, safe, and privacy-conscious participation standards.

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, provide actionable feedback, and protect the privacy of university users and data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intentional exposure of confidential data, and disruptive conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Do not include passwords, tokens, student records, or other sensitive data in reports.

Maintainers may clarify, mediate, remove harmful content, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
